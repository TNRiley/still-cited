The two lists disagree

Retraction Watch catalogues retraction notices, by hand. OpenAlex carries a is_retracted flag drawn from publisher metadata. They overlap, and they diverge — which matters if you are building a screening step that trusts either one alone.

These are the most-cited papers that one source flags and the other does not list. Some are plainly retracted and simply absent from the hand-curated list; at least one looks like a bad flag. Neither source is wrong so much as differently scoped.
